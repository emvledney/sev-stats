There are three material corrections.
1. The external data path finding (gm-8) is too optimistic for Android 14. Carlink's decompile-based analysis of the VCUUM-470.3 image shows GM's USB admission gate rejects unrecognized USB devices before apps can see them. A UsbManager USB-serial or USB-CAN adapter should be expected to fail once the truck is on UQBM. Owner reports also say the head unit cannot accept inbound sockets on its AP. The plan should make the head-unit app a network client: the laptop or Pi serves data over Wi-Fi (the car's AP or the laptop's own AP), with BLE or USB Ethernet as alternatives to validate.
2. The VHAL picture needs release-level precision. EV_BATTERY_AVERAGE_TEMPERATURE is Android 15 only, so it is unavailable on GM's 12L/14 builds. EV_CURRENT_BATTERY_CAPACITY needs Android 14. EV_BATTERY_INSTANTANEOUS_CHARGE_RATE is signed and may provide pack power if GM populates it while driving.

### NEW FACTS

- On GM AAOS 14 (VCUUM) production radios, a USB host admission gate rejects unrecognized USB devices before any app can see or request them. This likely kills UsbManager-based USB-serial/USB-CAN data paths once the truck is on Android 14.: The allow-list covers mass storage, MTP/ADB/phone-tablet, Android Auto's VID/PID, Apple iPhone, GM BCS/Aptiv hubs and generic hubs. UsbHostManager.usbDeviceAdded() rejects other devices and shows an 'Unsupported device' toast, so UsbManager.requestPermission is never reached. The gate is disabled only when ro.debuggable=1, so emulators and userdebug images won't reproduce it. It remembers one recently admitted serial number in memory; that is how Carlinkit's 'UDisk mode' toggle sneaks through. Plan implication: prefer Wi-Fi/TCP (app as client), BLE, or USB Ethernet (kernel netdev; unverified on A14) over USB-serial. Test on the real truck early. (https://github.com/lvalen91/carlink/blob/main/documents/reference/app-notes.md)

- On the car's Wi-Fi AP, the GM head unit reportedly cannot accept inbound sockets from hotspot clients. Working designs have the head-unit app dial OUT. The head unit can also join an external Wi-Fi network (e.g., a phone or laptop hotspot) as a client.: OAL: 'On the validated GM topology, the head unit cannot accept the phone's inbound projection socket directly', so the projection runs through a companion-owned proxy and the car app connects outward. OAL issue #47 logs the car app dialing a phone on the phone's hotspot (10.218.15.x) over ap_br_swlan0. OAL also says 'No paid vehicle data plan is required for projection'. For this project, run a TCP/WebSocket server or UDP broadcaster on the laptop or Pi and make the AAOS app the client. (https://github.com/mossyhub/openautolink/blob/main/docs/wireless-wpp.md https://github.com/mossyhub/openautolink/issues/47)

- VHAL property availability is gated by the Android release. EV_BATTERY_AVERAGE_TEMPERATURE (0x1160030E) exists only from Android 15. EV_CURRENT_BATTERY_CAPACITY (0x1160030D) exists from Android 14. EV_CHARGE_STATE (0x11400F41) and EV_CHARGE_TIME_REMAINING (0x11400F43) exist from Android 13. On GM's 12L (SQBRx) and 14 (UQBM) builds, pack temperature from VHAL is therefore impossible, and capacity/charge-state are possible only on UQBM.: Verified by grepping VehiclePropertyIds.java in android12L-release, android13-release, android14-release and main. EV_BATTERY_AVERAGE_TEMPERATURE carries @FlaggedApi(FLAG_ANDROID_VIC_VEHICLE_PROPERTIES). All of these need the dangerous CAR_ENERGY permission. (https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/services/Car/+/refs/heads/main/car-lib/src/android/car/VehiclePropertyIds.java https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/services/Car/+/refs/heads/android14-release/car-lib/src/android/car/VehiclePropertyIds.java https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/services/Car/+/refs/heads/android12L-release/car-lib/src/android/car/VehiclePropertyIds.java)

- EV_BATTERY_INSTANTANEOUS_CHARGE_RATE (0x1160030C) is signed: positive means charging, negative means discharging, in milliwatts. If GM populates it while driving, it gives HV battery power in/out without external hardware.: From the AOSP Javadoc. On the Blazer EV it read 0.0 W in Park; behavior while driving or DC fast charging on GM is unverified. Its change mode is CONTINUOUS, so subscribe at a modest rate. (https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/services/Car/+/refs/heads/main/car-lib/src/android/car/VehiclePropertyIds.java https://github.com/mossyhub/openautolink/blob/main/docs/ev-energy-model-reverse-engineering.md)
