# ultium-uds

## SUMMARY
Reading the Silverado EV's battery data through the OBD-II port looks feasible with an ordinary classic-CAN ELM327/STN-class adapter. Every public Ultium capture found (2024 Hummer EV, 2024 Lyriq, 2024 Equinox EV, 2024 BrightDrop, 2025 Lyriq, 2027 Bolt) talks 29-bit ISO 15765-4 at 500 kbit/s on DLC pins 6 and 14. The 11-bit IDs 0x7DF and 0x7E0–0x7E7, which include the Bolt EV battery module at 0x7E4/0x7EC, get no answer on Ultium, so Bolt-style tools and Car Scanner's Bolt profile show nothing. Ultium modules are addressed physically as 0x18DA<ecu>F1 → 0x18DAF1<ecu>. GM also uses a priority-0x14 form, 0x14DA<ecu>F1 → 0x142AF1<ecu>; this response ID is not standard ISO, but three independent sources observe it. Standard OBD requests go out functionally on 0x18DB33F1. Responding modules: 0xCB Battery System Manager (GM service name likely K16 BECM), 0x17/0x1D/0x1E drive-motor controllers, 0x40 body controller (it carries battery temperatures and charging data), 0x28 brakes and 0x45 gateway; 0xCD is a second battery manager that refuses every DID tried. Which priority works depends on the module: 0x40 answers only at 0x18, 0x28 only at 0x14, and 0xCB at both. A NO DATA reply often means the wrong priority, not a missing DID. DIDs confirmed on Ultium vehicles: 0xCB 22 27C6 (SoC, u16×100/65535), 27AF (energy remaining, u16/100 kWh), 27C7 (range, u24/103 mi), 2AF5 (average/min/max cell voltage, u16/10000 V each, plus bytes that by my own inference give the cell and module number of the extreme cells), 2B43 (26-byte per-module array that tracks SoC), 2AF1 (per-module temperatures, scaling disputed) and 5401 (charge-state code). From 0x17: 2885 (pack voltage, u16/100) and 2414 (pack current, s16/20 A, negative while charging). From 0x40: 434F (pack temperature, byte−40 °C), 4127/4124 (pack temperature sensors, u16/32 °C), 40E5/40E6 (coolant temperatures, u16/32 °C) and 4149 (charger pilot current). 33E5 gives the 12 V rail. No public source has a per-cell voltage array, SoH %, capacity in Ah, isolation resistance, contactor state or power limits for Ultium; one Hummer owner read 7,424 DIDs without finding a per-cell array. So the dashboard can show cell min/max/average with the location of the extreme cells, plus per-module values, but not all 96–192 cells. Usable kWh can be derived as 27AF ÷ (SoC/100), which gives about 192 kWh on the Hummer, and Ah can be estimated from coulomb counting with 2414. Passive sniffing at the DLC gets nothing: the gateway terminates and isolates the port, and the Hummer measured zero bytes on all four classic framings. Reaching internal buses would mean tapping wiring behind the gateway, which is ruled out. Reads in the default session need no authentication on 2024 vehicles, though a few drive-unit DIDs return 7F 22 33 (security access denied). One open risk could decide feasibility: 2025 Lyriq and 2025 Equinox EV captures got no answer from 0xCB, while 2024 vehicles and a 2027 Bolt asked at priority 0x14 did. The plan therefore has to probe both priorities and keep a fallback using 0x17 and 0x40. The Max Range pack is 24 modules of 8s3p 103 Ah NCMA pouch cells (576 cells): 96s6p at about 400 V while driving and 192s3p at about 800 V for DC fast charging. That is about 618 Ah in 400 V mode, about 345–355 V nominal and about 205 kWh usable. Extended Range is 20 modules (about 170 kWh) and Standard Range about 119 kWh; series counts for those two are unconfirmed. For safety, use only 0x22 plus OBD services 01/09 and DTC reads, one request at a time to physical addresses, never 0x10/0x11/0x14/0x27/0x28/0x2E/0x2F/0x31/0x34–0x38/0x3D/0x3E/0x85 or OBD 04. Poll at about 1–2 Hz for current and voltage and slower for the rest, and stop polling, keeping only the adapter-local ATRV check, when modules stop answering, so the always-powered DLC does not keep the gateway awake and drain the 12 V battery.

## FINDINGS

### [uds-1] (high, CRITICAL) Ultium (Global B) vehicles answer diagnostics at the DLC only on 29-bit ISO 15765-4 CAN at 500 kbit/s (ELM protocol ATSP7). 11-bit 0x7DF/0x7E0–0x7E7, including the Bolt BECM at 0x7E4/0x7EC, is not answered.
Hummer EV (2024, OBDLink MX+): the adapter auto-detected 29-bit/500k. OBDb PR #22 (Oct 2026): 'every 11-bit read ... was refused on the 2024 and 2025 [Lyriq] captures'. The 2024 Equinox EV support file lists 7E0/7E4 as unsupported. hacs_gm_ultium (2024 BrightDrop, Veepeak ELM327 v1.5 clone) reports ATDPN 7. A 2027 Bolt on Ultium/BEV3 (WiCAN #884) uses ATSP7;ATCP14. A Lyriq PR claimed 7E4/7EC and 18DA40F1 reach the same controller, but the later PR #22 contradicts it. Classic-CAN adapters are enough for reads; CAN FD at the DLC is not needed.

### [uds-2] (high, CRITICAL) Addressing: physical request 0x18DA<ecu>F1 → response 0x18DAF1<ecu> at priority 0x18. At priority 0x14 the request is 0x14DA<ecu>F1 and the response is 0x142AF1<ecu>, a GM-specific response ID. Standard OBD goes out functionally on 0x18DB33F1. Each module answers only at certain priorities.
Hummer CAN_PRIORITY.md / DEEP_SCAN.md: 0x17/0x1D/0x1E/0xCB answer at both 0x14 and 0x18. 0x28 answers only at 0x14 (7F 22 11 at 0x18). 0x40 answers only at 0x18 (NO DATA at 0x14, which earlier led the project to write the module off). The STN/ELM preamble used on the Hummer is ATCP<14|18>; ATSHDA<ecu>F1; ATCRA<rx id>; ATFCSH<pri>DA<ecu>F1; ATFCSD300000; ATFCSM1. The WiCAN bt1.json profile ('BT1: Hummer EV, Silverado EV, Sierra EV; BEV3 ...') uses ATSP7;ATCP14;ATSHDACBF1;ATCRA142AF1CB;ATFCSH14DACBF1;ATFCSD300000;ATFCSM1. OBDb Lyriq test cases show replies 142AF128... at priority 0x14 and 18DAF1CB... at 0x18. Some ELM clones reject the 4-byte ATSH 18DACBF1; the 3-byte ATSH DACBF1 plus ATSP7/ATCP works.

### [uds-5] (medium, CRITICAL) No public source has a per-cell voltage array for Ultium. Available: min/max/average cell voltage (2AF5) and per-module arrays (2B43, which tracks SoC, and 2AF1, which appears to be temperatures).
The Hummer owner scanned 0xCB ranges 2700–28FF and 2A00–2BFF plus 0x40/0x17/0x1D/0x1E/0x28: 7,424 reads, 912 answered. No captured array reproduces the 2AF5 statistics. 0xCD, the remaining candidate, has not been scanned. The Bolt per-cell-group DIDs (0x7E7 22 4181…) are 11-bit and not answered on Ultium. GM's deepest battery-data path is SPS2 'Hybrid/EV Battery Data Retrieval' on K16 (PIP6009B). It is a programming-system function and must not be used. A dashboard can show 96 or 192 cell positions only as min/max plus the location of the extreme cells.

### [uds-6] (medium) INFERENCE (mine): in the 10-byte 2AF5 payload, bytes 6–9 are two (cell number 1..N, module number) pairs that locate the extreme cells, with 8 series cells per module. They are not temperatures.
The rule module = floor((cell−1)/8)+1 holds in every public sample I checked. Lyriq examples: 5A/0C (cell 90 → module 12), 37/07 (55 → 7), 1E/04 (30 → 4), 4B/0A (75 → 10). Equinox EV: 2F/06 (47 → 6), 4B/0A, 42/09 (66 → 9), 14/03, 02/01, 06/01. Hummer: byte 9 stays at 23 while byte 8 reads 177–184, exactly the cells of module 23 in a 192-cell (800 V) numbering. The Hummer project read byte 8 as a possible temperature, which this pattern contradicts. Which pair is the min and which the max is unknown. Confirm on the truck before showing it as fact.
Sources: https://github.com/OBDb/Cadillac-LYRIQ/blob/main/tests/test_cases/2024/commands/DACB.222AF5.yaml https://github.com/OBDb/Chevrolet-Equinox-EV/blob/main/tests/test_cases/2024/commands/DACB.222AF5.yaml https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JeremyWhittaker/hummer_obdII/main/docs/PACK_ARCHITECTURE.md

### [uds-7] (medium) INFERENCE: 2B43 (26 data bytes) = [sub-pack A value][sub-pack B value or 00][up to 24 per-module SoC-like values].
Lyriq (12 modules): 87 00 then 12 values, then zeros. Equinox EV: B0 00 then 10 values. Hummer (24 modules in two 12-module layers): all 26 bytes non-zero; positions 2–13 and 14–25 form two distinct blocks; the per-position pattern repeats across 80 sessions; r=+0.999 with SoC and r≈0 with cell spread, so these are not cell voltages. OBDb decodes byte 0 as ×100/255 %. The Hummer fit is 0.4324/count −5.58, which is not an exact scaling. BrightDrop returned a malformed ISO-TP length for this 29-byte reply, so use 27C6 for SoC. Counting non-zero bytes 2..25 should give the module count (24 on a Max Range pack).

### [uds-8] (medium, CRITICAL) Feasibility risk: model-year 2025 Lyriq and Equinox EV captures got no answer from 0xCB enhanced reads (27C6/2AF5/2B43), while every 2024 vehicle answered and a 2027 Bolt answered at priority 0x14. The cause (gateway filtering vs CAN priority vs wake state) is unknown.
The Lyriq PR #13/#14 author (2025, VIN 1GYKPTRL5S…) says 0xCB answers J1979 0100 but not UDS 22. That PR says it probed 'all 256 physical 29-bit addresses' and concluded a gateway filter, but PR #22 notes 0xCB's answers 'depend on wake mode'. The 2025 Equinox shows 0xCB answering functional 0142 (18DAF1CB 04 41 42 …) while DACB 22-reads are listed unsupported. The OBDb DACB entries carry no priority byte, so priority 0x18 is assumed; whether 0x14 was tried is not stated. WiCAN #884 (2027 Bolt, BEV3, Aug 2026) reports 2227C6 etc. working with ATCP14 and ATCRA142AF1CB. On the 2025 Lyriq, 0x17 (2414) and 0x40 (temperatures, pilot current) still answered, so a reduced dashboard exists even if 0xCB stays silent. Silverado EV modules get OTA updates (e.g., GM Update 661.10 for VICM, MY2024–2026), so DID availability could change over time (inference).

### [uds-11] (medium) DLC pinout on Global B: pins 6/14 are the diagnostic CAN pair (terminated at the SDGM); pin 16 is unswitched +12 V; pins 4/5 are grounds. Per secondary sources, pin 1 carries a secondary CAN-FD network not used for diagnostics, and four pins carry Ethernet (DoIP). Exact Ethernet and pin 9 assignments are unverified.

The DLC is fed by IP fuse block (right) F14 'DLC' on the Hummer. The OBD port is permanently powered (hacs_gm_ultium). The Hummer project declined to listen on pin 1 because its bus and bit rate are unidentified. Only pins 4, 5, 6, 14 and 16 should be used.

### [uds-13] (high, CRITICAL) Safe set: UDS 0x22 in the default session to physical addresses, OBD 01/02/09, and DTC reads 03/07/0A addressed to 0x45. Everything that changes state is forbidden. A 7,424-read parked scan on a Hummer set no DTCs.
Hummer forbidden list: OBD 04 and 08; UDS 10, 11, 14, 27, 28, 2E, 2F, 31, 34–38, 3B, 3D, 3E, 83, 84, 85, 87. NRC handling: 0x31 = DID not implemented; 0x33 or 0x34 = locked, record and move on; 0x7E/0x7F = needs another session, never send 0x10; 0x78 = response pending, wait; 0x21 = busy, stop rather than retry; 0x11 at a priority = wrong priority. Speed read zero before every scan read and the DTC brackets stayed at zero across all 7,424 reads. TesterPresent (0x3E) is unnecessary in the default session. 0x23/0x2A/0x2C/0xAA are read-adjacent but change ECU behaviour or are security-sensitive; exclude them (my recommendation).

### [uds-14] (medium) Polling: one outstanding request at a time with at least 50–100 ms gaps, each DID once per cycle, about 1–2 Hz only for pack current and voltage. When modules go silent, back off to adapter-local ATRV so the always-on DLC does not keep the gateway awake.
hacs_gm_ultium: defaults of 30 s awake and 5 min parked. Querying the same DID several times per cycle made the BEC time out, so derive min/max from one 2AF5 reply. Polling can keep the gateway and BEC awake, which draws far more than the dongle's tens of mA; suggested 12 V guard 12.0–12.2 V, to be measured. Hummer: about 5.5 s per full row; ATRV only while asleep; the truck wakes by itself every couple of hours; NO DATA while asleep is 'a wait condition, never a reason to probe harder'; the scanner used 75 ms extra delay; Service 01 can sleep even while charging. OBDb suggested frequencies: 27C6 0.5 s, 2AF5/2B43/2414 1 s, 0x40 temperatures 5 s. GM CSP N242435630 shows a Global B serial data gateway was reflashed for 12 V drain after OTA (2024 ICE trucks), so 12 V is a known sensitivity.

### [uds-17] (high) Adapters: classic-CAN STN or ELM devices that support ATSP7, ATCP, ATCRA (29-bit) and ATFCSH/ATFCSD/ATFCSM have been proven. CAN FD hardware and MDI 2/GDS2 are unnecessary for reading.
Proven: OBDLink MX+ (STN2255, classic CAN only; Hummer); Veepeak BLE ELM327 v1.5 clone (BrightDrop; rejects 4-byte ATSH); ELM327-class via an RPi BLE bridge (2025 Lyriq; auto-detect failed, ATSP7 forced); WiCAN (bt1.json profile). The Hummer project rejected CAN FD interfaces (red panda, PiCAN FD, Waveshare) because no reachable FD segment was identified. It also judged MDI 2 + GDS2 (Techline Connect, CAN FD/DoIP) a bidirectional dealer tool, unsuitable for unattended use. OBDX Pro GT: CAN FD is needed on some 2024+ GM vehicles for programming and security actions, not for reads.

## RECOMMENDATIONS
- Use a classic-CAN STN-based adapter on pins 6/14 only. OBDLink MX+ (BT Classic) has been proven on a Hummer EV; OBDLink CX or a Veepeak BLE also work. CAN FD hardware is not needed. Never connect to pin 1, the Ethernet pins or any internal harness.
- Build a hard allowlist into the app's transport layer: only `22 xxxx` for an exact list of (ECU, priority, DID) tuples to physical 29-bit addresses, OBD 01/09 functional, and 03/07/0A to 0x45. The transport should refuse 04, 08, 10, 11, 14, 27, 28, 29, 2E, 2F, 31, 34–38, 3B, 3D, 3E, 83–87, and never send 22 functionally.
- First on-truck session (parked, Ready mode, attended, laptop + adapter): ATZ/ATE0/ATH1/ATRV/ATSP7. Then 0100 and 090A on 18DB33F1 to take the ECU census, a DTC baseline (03/07/0A to 0x45), and single probes of CB:27C6 at ATCP18 and ATCP14, 17:2885 and 17:2414, CB:2AF5, CB:2B43 and 40:434F. Log raw frames byte-exact and re-read DTCs at the end.
- If 0xCB is silent at both priorities, which may happen on MY2025/2026, fall back to 17:2414/2885 for power and 0x40 (434F, 4127, 4124, 40E5, 40E6, 4149) for temperatures. Use OBD 01 for speed and 12 V, and SoC from 0x40 or the infotainment's own properties if another topic finds them.
- Derive pack structure at runtime rather than hard-coding it: series count = round(2885 V ÷ 2AF5 average); module count = non-zero bytes 2..25 of 2B43. Show 'usable kWh (est.)' = 27AF ÷ (27C6/100) and 'Ah (est.)' from coulomb counting with 2414 during a stable charge. Label both as estimates.
- Present cell data honestly. Show min/max/average and spread (mV) from 2AF5, plus the extreme-cell locations as 'cell N, module M' marked experimental. Show per-module bars from 2B43 and 2AF1 as relative deviations until the scalings are confirmed.
- Poll in tiers: fast 0.5–1 s for current and voltage, 1–2 s for SoC and cell stats, 5–10 s for temperatures, 30–60 s for energy and range. Keep requests strictly serial with at least 50–100 ms gaps and one request per DID per cycle. Back off to adapter-only ATRV when modules return NO DATA, and stop entirely below about 12.0–12.2 V or when the head unit sleeps.
- Do any new-DID discovery only parked and attended, at slow pace (≥75 ms extra delay), with speed=0 checks and DTC bracketing. Stop on 7F xx 21, bus errors or a new DTC, and never pursue 7F 22 33/34/7E/7F.
- Do not use SPS2 'Hybrid/EV Battery Data Retrieval' or any GDS2 function beyond Data Display. If parameter names are needed, a short ACDelco TDS / GDS2 Data Display session on the K16 BECM lists can map names to DIDs, kept separate from the app.
- Treat the 2024 Hummer EV project (JeremyWhittaker/hummer_obdII) and OBDb Lyriq/Equinox data as the reference implementations. Consider contributing confirmed Silverado EV captures back to OBDb/Chevrolet-Silverado-EV, whose signalset is currently empty.
