# play-distribution

## SUMMARY
You can't adb-install onto a production car. Google's docs say a real vehicle only runs apps installed from a trusted source such as Google Play, and they name Internal App Sharing or an internal test track as the no-review routes. The distribution table limits Internal App Sharing to Android Auto, which leaves the Play internal testing track as the practical path for AAOS. Internal testing gets no car-quality form-factor review: none for internal, non-blocking for closed, blocking for open and production. It allows up to 100 testers named by email and is not blocked by the rule that new personal accounts must run a 12-tester, 14-day closed test, because that rule only gates production.

Setup path in Play Console: Test and release > Advanced settings > Form factors > + Add form factor > Android Automotive OS > Manage, then choose the dedicated 'Automotive OS only' release track. This is required for templated apps and cannot be undone once saved. Then upload an AAB to Test and release > Testing > Internal testing; through the Publishing API the track is 'automotive:qa'. Add the same Google account that is signed in on the truck as a tester and opt in through the link on the Testers tab.

Builds reach testers within minutes. Internal-test apps are not searchable, and two community sources (dated 2023–2026) report installing from the Play website on a computer, choosing the car as the target device. On GM vehicles, Google Play access is tied to an OnStar data plan.

AAOS Play categories are Media, Navigation, POI (including EV charging), IoT and Weather for use while driving, plus parked-only Video, Games and Browsers (labs). There is no 'vehicle stats' category. The closest fit is IoT, which is what Car Stats Viewer's Play Edition declares; POI also fits if charging features are added. For internal testing to yourself the category is not reviewed, so an app outside every category can still be distributed. Car Stats Viewer's activity-based 'Legacy' build has shipped this way on internal tracks for years.

Driver distraction: by default the OS covers any activity without distractionOptimized="true" whenever UX restrictions are active. The platform only honours that tag for apps installed from an allowed installer such as Play, or on userdebug builds. That is confirmed in the AOSP CarPackageManagerService source and in Google's CarAppActivity docs. Google rejects the tag on any activity other than CarAppActivity at review, but internal tests are not reviewed, and Car Stats Viewer's DO-tagged Legacy dashboard runs while driving on Polestar and Volvo.

Templated (Car App Library) screens have hard limits:
- At most 5 templates per task.
- A refresh only escapes that quota if the title, row count and row titles stay the same.
- Default content limits are 6 list rows, 6 grid items and 4 pane rows, with 2 lines of text per pane row; a pane can also carry one image of up to 480×480 dp.
- The host throttles screen updates. A Polestar developer measured about 1 s per update before a June 2024 host update and about 7–8 s after it.
- Custom drawing on a Surface needs ACCESS_SURFACE plus NAVIGATION_TEMPLATES or MAP_TEMPLATES, which Google intends only for navigation, POI and weather apps.

Best rendering approach:
- **Parked:** a full Compose activity without the DO tag, so the OS blocks it automatically while driving.
- **Driving, guideline-compliant:** a templated IoT screen with about 4–6 rows of key figures, where values change in the row text and titles stay fixed.
- **Driving, technically possible but against guidelines (internal-only, at your own safety judgement):** a DO-tagged custom activity, or Surface drawing through NavigationTemplate or MapWithContentTemplate.

Nothing found confirms that internal-track installs, or the DO tag, work specifically on GM head units. Third-party Play car apps do run on GM: ABRP's navigation app on a GMC Sierra EV, and Aximote's public car-data app, whose listing names Chevrolet, Cadillac and GMC. Car Stats Viewer's GitHub issues contain no GM, Chevrolet, Cadillac or GMC reports.

Current Play rules:
- New apps must be AABs signed with Play App Signing (long-standing rule, recalled rather than re-fetched).
- AAOS apps must target API 35 or higher from Aug 31, 2026; mobile apps need API 36.
- New personal accounts must verify a real Android phone through the Play Console app before publishing; the page doesn't say whether this applies to internal testing.

### [pd-1] (high, CRITICAL) Internal testing gets no car-quality (form-factor) review; closed testing review is non-blocking; open testing and production are blocking.

### [pd-2] (high, CRITICAL) Production cars only run apps from a trusted source such as Google Play. Use an internal test track; Internal App Sharing is listed for Android Auto only.
Google's testing page says real-vehicle testing requires installing from a trusted source such as Google Play, and names Internal App Sharing or an Internal Test Track as the ways to skip review. The 'Unknown sources' developer option exists only for Android Auto. The distribute page's review table labels Internal sharing as '(Android Auto only)'. Car Stats Viewer's BUILD.md says apps cannot be sideloaded onto a production car and must go through Play, with an internal test as the personal-use route. So the laptop is a build and upload machine, not an adb install path to the GM head unit. A third-party file manager (AnExplorer) claims USB APK sideloading on GM, but that is unverified marketing, and sideloaded apps would not get their DO tag honoured (see pd-9).

### [pd-7] (high) AAOS Play categories: Media, Navigation, POI, IoT and Weather (usable while driving), plus Video, Games and Browsers (parked-only; Browsers is labs, internal track only). There is no vehicle-data or dashboard category.

### [pd-8] (high) Under review, IoT/POI/Navigation/Weather apps on AAOS may only use their own activities for setup, settings and sign-in while parked (PE-1). Everything else must be templated, and images and animation are restricted (IU-1, SA-1).
Car-optimized tier: PE-1 says that except for setup, settings and sign-in flows while parked, the app must not provide any functionality through its own activities. SA-1 bans animated elements, with an exception for canvas animations relevant to driving while parked. IU-1 bans images except specific exceptions. AC-1 requires tasks to be completable in 5 screens or fewer. Since August 2026, EP-4 requires AAOS screenshots for these categories. These only bite at closed (non-blocking), open and production review.

### [pd-9] (high, CRITICAL) The platform only honours the distractionOptimized tag for non-system apps installed by an allowed installer such as Play, or on userdebug/eng builds. Play-installed internal-test apps therefore can show a custom activity while driving, but Google rejects any DO activity other than CarAppActivity at review.
AOSP CarPackageManagerService (main): on user builds, non-system apps whose installer is missing from the OEM's R.array.allowedAppInstallSources are skipped with 'not installed from permitted sources'. The comment reads 'This prevents side-loaded apps to fake DO.' The meta-data key is the string 'distractionOptimized'; there is also an optional 'android.car.drivingsafetyregions' tag. Google's templated-app doc warns that DO activities are 'only treated as such when installed from a trusted source (such as the Google Play Store) or when running on a userdebug system image'. The AAOS overview says only CarAppActivity may carry the tag, or the app is rejected. AOSP's guidelines page says the platform 'can't detect or enforce' actual adherence; that happens at Play review. Precedent: Car Stats Viewer's maintainer wrote that the DO-tagged 'Legacy Dashboard has been the standard while-driving screen in the internal test builds' on Polestar and Volvo. GM's allowedAppInstallSources and activity denylist overlay values are unknown.
Sources: https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/services/Car/+/refs/heads/main/service/src/com/android/car/pm/CarPackageManagerService.java https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/services/Car/+/refs/heads/main/service/res/values/config.xml https://developer.android.com/training/cars/apps/automotive-os https://developer.android.com/training/cars/platforms/automotive-os https://source.android.com/docs/automotive/driver_distraction/guidelines https://github.com/Ixam97/CarStatsViewer/issues/225

### [pd-10] (high) Activities without the DO tag are blocked automatically while UX restrictions are active: the OS covers them and moves them to Paused (or Stopped in compatibility mode). That makes a full Compose dashboard a safe, zero-risk parked-only screen.
From the parked apps doc: 'By default, activities cannot be used or launched while UX restrictions are active'; a resumed activity 'is obscured by an activity owned by the OS'. OEMs set the rules, which can vary by region and by display. Read CarUxRestrictionsManager.getCurrentCarUxRestrictions().isRequiresDistractionOptimization() rather than inferring restrictions from gear or speed. The car-ready parked categories (Video, Games, Browsers) rely on this same mechanism (DD-2, DD-3).
Sources: https://developer.android.com/training/cars/parked/automotive-os https://developer.android.com/training/cars/platforms/automotive-os

### [pd-11] (high) Car App Library limits: at most 5 templates per task, and a refresh is only exempt when the title, row count and row titles are unchanged. Default limits are 6 list rows, 6 grid items and 4 pane rows; panes allow 2 text lines per row, 2 actions and one image up to 480 dp. The host throttles updates.
Template restrictions: 5 templates per task, and the last must be Navigation, Pane, Message, MediaPlayback, SignIn or LongMessage; exceeding the quota makes the host show an error and close the app. PaneTemplate counts as a refresh if it was loading, or if the title and the number and titles of rows are unchanged. ListTemplate also requires the same section structure. So put changing values in Row.addText(), never in titles. Defaults from androidx integers.xml: content_limit_list=6, grid=6, place_list=6, route_list=3, pane=4; read the real values with ConstraintManager.getContentLimit(), where CONTENT_LIMIT_TYPE_PANE=4. PaneTemplate.build(): rows may have up to 2 text lines and no Toggle or OnClickListener, with up to 2 Actions. Pane.Builder.setImage (API 4) takes a 480x480 dp image, so a server-side-rendered gauge bitmap is technically possible. Screen.onGetTemplate: 'the host will throttle template updates'. Field data: Car Stats Viewer saw refreshes slow from about 1 s to about 7–8 s after a June 2024 Automotive Template Host update that moved to API 7; still about 8 s in Aug 2024.
Sources: https://developer.android.com/training/cars/apps/library/template-restrictions https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/car/app/model/PaneTemplate https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/car/app/model/PaneTemplate.Builder https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/car/app/model/ListTemplate https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/car/app/Screen https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/car/app/constraints/ConstraintManager https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/+/refs/heads/androidx-main/car/app/app/src/main/res/values/integers.xml https://github.com/Ixam97/CarStatsViewer/issues/225

### [pd-12] (medium) Free-form drawing on a Surface (Canvas, or VirtualDisplay with Presentation/Compose) is only offered to navigation, POI and weather apps. It needs ACCESS_SURFACE plus NAVIGATION_TEMPLATES (NavigationTemplate) or MAP_TEMPLATES (MapWithContentTemplate, API 7).
The draw-maps doc says navigation, POI and weather apps can draw maps by accessing a Surface. MapWithContentTemplate takes NAVIGATION_TEMPLATES or MAP_TEMPLATES; NavigationTemplate takes NAVIGATION_TEMPLATES; ACCESS_SURFACE is always required in addition. Rendering Compose through VirtualDisplay and Presentation into the surface is documented. CarAppPermission javadoc: an app outside the permitted categories requesting these 'may be rejected upon submission', which is review-time enforcement. Quality guideline NF-2 limits navigation surfaces to map content. Car Stats Viewer's dev flavor declares NAVIGATION_TEMPLATES and ACCESS_SURFACE while its CarAppService declares IOT, and renders real-time plots into NavigationTemplate when carAppApiLevel is 7 or higher. That points to no strict runtime category check by the host, but this is inferred, not documented.

## RECOMMENDATIONS
- Distribute only through the Play internal testing track on the dedicated 'Automotive OS only' track, with the user's own Google account (the one signed in on the truck) as the sole tester. Never promote to closed, open or production: no review applies on internal, and the 12-tester production gate doesn't matter. Don't plan on adb installs to the GM head unit; the laptop builds the AAB and uploads it, either in the Play Console web UI or through the Publishing API with track 'automotive:qa'.
- Before writing the real app, run a one-day feasibility spike. Publish a minimal app to the AAOS internal track (package e.g. com.<you>.silveradostats). It should contain: a templated IOT CarAppService showing carAppApiLevel, INFO_MAKE/MODEL/MODEL_YEAR and a 1 Hz counter in a PaneTemplate row's text, to measure the host throttle; a parked-only Compose activity without the DO tag; and a second Compose activity tagged DO. Install it on the truck through play.google.com from the laptop, signed in with the same account. While parked, record what launches. Have a passenger observe the screen while someone else drives, to see which surfaces stay visible.
- Play Console order of operations:
1. Complete account identity checks and phone device verification.
2. Create the app.
3. Go to Test and release > Advanced settings > Form factors > + Add form factor > Android Automotive OS > Manage, and pick the dedicated track. This is irreversible, which is fine.
4. Upload a signed AAB to Testing > Internal testing for the Automotive OS track.
5. Agree to the review policy and add 2 portrait and 2 landscape screenshots from the generic AAOS emulator to avoid opt-in blockers.
6. Add the tester email list and open the opt-in link.
7. Install on the truck from the web, then let Play auto-update new builds while parked.
- Build settings: compileSdk/targetSdk 36 (meets AAOS API 35 and mobile API 36); minSdk 29 or the truck's API level, whichever is higher; androidx.car.app:app and app-automotive 1.7.0 stable; useLibrary("android.car"); uses-feature automotive and templates_host required=true; wifi, portrait, landscape and camera required=false; AAB with Play App Signing.
- UI plan.
- Parked: a full-density Compose dashboard (per-cell voltages, temperatures, kWh/Ah, charge curve) in an activity without the DO tag; the OS blocks it automatically while driving.
- Driving, guideline-compliant: a templated IOT PaneTemplate or ListTemplate with about 4–6 fixed-title rows (pack V, A, kW, SoC, average battery temperature, outside temperature), values in Row.addText(), app-side updates capped at 1 Hz, and no claim of real-time fidelity.
- Driving, internal-only and against Google's guidelines: either a DO-tagged Compose activity, or Surface rendering through MapWithContentTemplate/NavigationTemplate with ACCESS_SURFACE. Ship these only as a separate opt-in build flavor, after the user consciously accepts the driver-distraction trade-off; default to the compliant view.
- Make the app driven by its UX-restriction listener: watch CarUxRestrictionsManager.isRequiresDistractionOptimization() and degrade content gracefully, rather than inferring driving from gear or speed. Keep the data-collection service separate from the UI so the parked and driving screens share one read-only data layer. That layer only reads properties: no setProperty calls and no vendor or system permissions, which satisfies the constraint not to impair any truck functionality.
- Before committing, confirm an active OnStar or connected-services data plan on the truck. GM ties Google Play access to it, so without one nothing in this plan can be installed.
- Use the GM OEM emulator image (developer.gm.com/in-vehicle-apps; it needs a browser) and the generic API 35-ext15 AAOS image without the Play Store to develop and test UX-restriction behaviour locally. Remember that DO activities only display while driving on Play Store images when the app was installed from Play.
- Set expectations with the user: Car Stats Viewer's maintainer found AAOS exposes little BMS data beyond SoC and power on Polestar. Whether GM exposes cell voltages, Ah capacity or pack current to third-party apps belongs to the data-source research topic. If it doesn't, the Play distribution path still holds, but the dashboard will need another read-only data source (e.g. a BLE OBD dongle). That source would need its own permission and safety review.
