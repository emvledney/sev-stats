### NEW FACTS

- An independent, safety-gated Hummer EV (BT1) telemetry project gives the most complete public evidence for Ultium trucks: classic CAN through an OBDLink MX+, measured addressing for each module, and a 7,424-DID read-only scan.: JeremyWhittaker/hummer_obdII (2026): Pi Zero 2 W + OBDLink MX+ over RFCOMM, with an allowlist that blocks writes. Validated (level 4): BEC 0xCB 0x27C6 SoC, 0x27AF energy kWh, 0x2AF5 cell avg/min/max/spread; DMCM 0x17 0x2885 pack V and 0x2414 pack A (0x17 and 0x1D agree to +0.032 V mean). The pack is about 96s (96 × 3.9963 = 383.6 V vs 383.9 V measured). Candidates: BCM 0x40 at priority 0x18 holds 0x40E5/0x40E6 coolant and 0x434F HV temperature; BEC 0x2709 compressor temperature; 0x0046 pack temperature. Scan: 912 hits; BEC payloads up to 448 bytes (0x2B74), 24-long single-byte runs at 0x276E-0x2785 and 0x2793-0x27AA (module count). The per-cell voltage array is still unidentified, and the second BMS, 0xCD, refuses all IDs tried (7F 22 31). Charger AC/DC IDs ported from the Bolt return NRC 0x31 on a 2025 LYRIQ.
